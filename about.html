Our story

Gardening should feel like a deep breath.

SimpliGarden is a small, independent guide for people who want to grow things without turning it into another performance. Less stuff, more noticing. Less perfect, more alive.

We write for balconies, rented courtyards, kitchen windowsills, and the slightly overgrown bit at the end of the garden. The places where real life happens.

“Start small. Stay curious. Eat the salad.”

How we got here

It began with one sad basil plant.

And then another. And then a windowsill full of pots that were either thriving or quietly giving up, depending on the week. What helped was not a new gadget or a stricter routine. It was learning a few simple rules and then being allowed to break them.

SimpliGarden exists to pass that feeling on: the relief of understanding what a plant actually needs, the pleasure of a seed coming up, and the freedom to garden in the time and space you really have.

Every guide is tested in ordinary conditions. We tell you what matters, what can wait, and what to do when the slugs win. Because they will, sometimes. It is still worth it.

What we believe

Three things
we keep coming back to.

These are the rules behind every guide, planner page, and slightly over-enthusiastic seed order.

✿

Small is a strategy.

A single pot is not a lesser garden. It is a place to pay attention. Start with what you can water, reach, and enjoy without resentment.

☼

Seasonal, not stressful.

There is a right time for most jobs, and it is rarely “right now, perfectly.” Follow the rhythm loosely. The garden will forgive you.

❋

Leave room for wildness.

A tidy garden is not the only beautiful one. Seedheads, uneven edges, and a few unplanned volunteers feed wildlife and make better stories.

“The garden is not a test you can fail. It is a conversation that keeps going.”
