SimpliGarden (“SimpliGarden”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, read our guides, use our contact form, download the planner, or join our newsletter.

A note before you read: this is a general website template, not legal advice. Before launching or relying on this policy, have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer and make sure it matches the tools and providers you actually use.

1. Overview

We keep things simple. We collect only the information we need to run the site, answer your messages, and send the newsletter if you ask for it. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use advertising cookies.

For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, SimpliGarden is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You can contact us using the details at the end of this page.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, topic, and message. The form opens your email app with the message ready to send; we receive only what you choose to send.
  • Newsletter: your email address and any preferences you share when you subscribe.
  • Messages and feedback: anything you send us by email, social media, or another channel, including photos you choose to share.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, and the pages you visit. Our hosting provider may record this in server logs to keep the site secure and working.
  • Usage data: aggregated, non-identifying information about how visitors use the site, such as which guides are popular. We only collect this if analytics are enabled.

Information from third parties

If you subscribe through a newsletter provider or contact us through a social platform, that provider may share your email address, name, and subscription status with us. We only use that information for the purpose you gave it to us.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • reply to your questions, feedback, and privacy requests;
  • send the newsletter and planner if you ask for them;
  • keep the site secure, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse;
  • understand which guides and pages are useful so we can improve them;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms; and
  • protect our rights and the rights of our visitors.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling in a way that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

4. Legal bases

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: for sending the newsletter and for any non-essential cookies or analytics. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract: where we need to process information to provide a service you have asked for, such as sending a planner you requested.
  • Legitimate interests: for responding to enquiries, keeping the site secure, improving our content, and running a small gardening publication, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to keep or share information to comply with the law.

5. Cookies and local storage

We aim to keep tracking to a minimum. This site does not use advertising cookies or sell data to ad networks.

We may use:

  • Essential storage: small amounts of local storage needed to remember your preferences or keep the site working.
  • Fonts: we load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font, Google may receive your IP address and technical information about your device. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Analytics: if we enable analytics, we will use a privacy-conscious provider and update this policy. Analytics will not be used to identify you personally.
  • Newsletter form: on this demonstration version of the site, the newsletter form stores the email address you enter in your browser's local storage and does not transmit it to a server. If a newsletter provider is connected, this policy will be updated before launch.

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect how parts of the site work.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers: hosting, email delivery, analytics, and other suppliers who help us run the site, under contracts that require them to protect your information.
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, accountants, and insurers, where necessary and subject to confidentiality.
  • Authorities: where required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • A successor organisation: if we merge, sell, or transfer part of our business, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes described above:

  • Contact messages: usually up to 24 months after we close the enquiry, unless we need to keep them longer for a legal or contractual reason.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, plus a short period to process the request and maintain suppression records.
  • Server logs: usually up to 12 months, unless needed to investigate a security incident.
  • Aggregated analytics: retained in non-identifying form for as long as useful.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before you withdrew it; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise a right, email hello@simpligarden.com with the subject line “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity before we can respond. We will aim to reply within one month, or within the period required by local law.

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in each email.

9. Children

Our site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

We are based in the United Kingdom. Some of our service providers may process information outside the UK or European Economic Area. Where that happens, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision, to protect your information.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, secure hosting, and limiting who can see your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and any relevant authority as required by law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, providers, or the law. The date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect. If a change is material, we will make reasonable efforts to give notice on the site or by email.

13. Contact

If you have a question about this policy or want to exercise a privacy right, contact us at hello@simpligarden.com or use the contact form.
