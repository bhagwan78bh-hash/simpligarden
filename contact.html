Good questions

Before you ask.

A few things people ask most often. If yours is not here, the form above is the place.

Do I need a garden to use SimpliGarden?

Not at all. Most of our guides work on a balcony, a windowsill, or a small patio. Start with the small-space filter in the guide library.

Are the guides suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Every guide assumes you are starting from zero, explains the supplies in plain language, and includes a realistic time estimate. No specialist kit required.

Can I republish or use your guides?

You are welcome to link to our guides and share short quotes with credit. For anything more substantial, please ask first. The full rules are in our terms of use.

How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us and we will remove you. See our privacy policy for more about how we handle your details.

Do you accept guest posts or collaborations?

Sometimes. Send a short note with what you have in mind, who it is for, and any relevant links. We are most interested in practical, honest gardening stories.
